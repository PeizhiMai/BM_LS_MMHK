## Task 1 (highest priority): define the MMHK model in the manuscript

The draft's Eq. (1) is the plain Hubbard Hamiltonian; the only description of the actual
method is one sentence ("keeps the interaction local in the momentum-space cluster basis
while allowing momentum mixing beyond the purely diagonal HK limit"). A referee cannot
evaluate the paper without the model. Add a subsection in "Model and diagnostics" with:

1. The explicit MMHK Hamiltonian: how the Brillouin zone is partitioned into 4x4
   cluster-momentum patches, how the Hubbard U (and the nearest-neighbor V) interaction is
   projected so it is local in the cluster basis, and what inter-cluster terms are dropped.
   Source the definition from the actual solver code (look in `code/`, `2x2test/`,
   `gse_analysis/` inputs, and the sibling project `../Violation_Luttinger` if needed;
   `2x2test/calculate_2x2.ipynb` and `find_observables_doped_clean_2x2.py` show the
   construction at 2x2).
2. The two limits: cluster 1x1 = pure Hatsugai-Kohmoto; cluster = full lattice = exact
   Hubbard. State where 4x4 sits and why the construction is exactly solvable in the
   thermodynamic limit (momenta couple only within a cluster).
3. Whether the construction is variational, and the precise relation between "4x4-MMHK"
   and (a) exact 4x4 Hubbard ED, (b) the thermodynamic-limit Hubbard model. Note: the
   data show MMHK energies BELOW TDL AFQMC (by 0.009-0.017t), whereas exact 4x4 PBC ED
   energies lie ABOVE TDL. So 4x4-MMHK is not the same thing as 4x4 ED, and the current
   "smooth offset" language conflates finite-size and truncation errors (see Task 3).
4. Methods detail currently missing: how G(k, omega) is computed (Lanczos; specify
   iterations/convergence), the broadening eta = 0.05 used in the main figures, the
   frequency grid, the high-symmetry-path interpolation, the criterion for extracting the
   zero of Re G(k, 0), and how the V term is handled in the cluster-momentum basis.
5. Appendix figure on broadening robustness: eta = 0.05 / 0.1 / 0.2 figures already exist
   (`figures/luttinger_tp0_eta0p{05,1,2}.pdf`, same for tpn25). Add a short appendix
   showing the extracted zero dispersion is eta-insensitive.

## Task 2 (the science upgrade): promote the finite-V results and test the V ~ U/4 reversal

`eta0p05_V_effect_luttinger_dispersion_metrics.csv` shows the affine scale of the
finite-V Luttinger-surface dispersion relative to V=0 is NON-monotonic, and the reversal
point tracks U/4 in all three datasets:

| U  | scale_vs_V0 > 1 up to | < 1 from | U/4 |
|----|----------------------|----------|-----|
| 8  | V=2 (1.247)          | V=3 (0.907) | 2  |
| 20 | V=4 (1.190)          | V=6 (0.927) | 5  |
| 40 | V=8 (1.207)          | V=12 (0.912)| 10 |

V = U/z with z = 4 is the mean-field Mott-to-CDW boundary of the half-filled extended
Hubbard model. If the reversal is a real transition signature, the finite-V section
upgrades from "the zero surface shifts smoothly" to "the Luttinger surface detects the
Mott-to-charge-order transition" - this should become the paper's climax.

Steps:

1. **Verify it is not an extraction artifact.** The U=8 delta-epsilon curves
   (right column of `eta0p05_V_effect_luttinger_dispersion_comparison.png`) are noisy and
   staircase-like, suggesting the root finder jumps between multiple zeros of Re G(k,0).
   Modify the extraction in `scripts/analyze_v_effect_luttinger_dispersion.py` (and its
   upstream in `scripts/reproduce_luttinger_figures.py`) to (a) track ALL sign changes of
   Re G along omega near 0, (b) follow the same root continuously along the k-path, and
   (c) flag k-points where the number of zeros changes. Re-generate the U=8 panel.
2. **Characterize what changes at the boundary.** For each (U, V): ground-state total
   momentum sector, charge structure factor at (pi, pi) (CDW order proxy), charge gap,
   and zero-contour location at omega = 0. Determine whether the scale reversal coincides
   with a level crossing / sector change. Ground-state data live under `gse_analysis/`
   and `data/Gcalpath/`; if the CDW structure factor requires new measurements, queue them
   via the existing workflow in `scripts/cades_queue/`.
3. **Refine the V grid near the boundary** for at least one U (e.g. U=20: V = 4.5, 5.0,
   5.5) to locate the reversal and compare with V = U/4.
4. **Write the section**: replace the boxed placeholder `fig:ls_vscan_placeholder` and the
   `\resultplaceholder` with a real figure: spectra + zero contours across the scan, plus
   a summary panel of scale_vs_V0 (and the CDW proxy) versus V/U showing the collapse of
   the reversal point at V/U = 1/4. State clearly whether the zero contour evolves
   continuously or reconstructs.
5. If the reversal turns out to be an artifact, report the smooth V-evolution honestly
   and keep the corrected extraction; the section still completes the paper.

## Task 3: clean up the benchmark logic (separate finite-size from truncation error)
